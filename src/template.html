How the chart reads the file

  • Each block is a step; the small number is its place in the file. A step carries on from the one before it in its section unless that one named a product (rule 9).
  • -> name names what a step or section makes; @&name{} later pulls it in. That is the only way a branch joins the main line (rules 1–8).
  • The right-hand column is the longest line to the dish; everything else flows into it from the left. A product used twice is drawn once; the second use shows “from step N”.
  • Steps with nothing going in at the top of the recipe (grease the tin, preheat) sit above the chart. Bold words are the step actions written in backticks.

Your browser parses each recipe with cooklang-rs (the spec-2.0 branch) compiled to WebAssembly. The syntax is in three open proposals to the Cooklang spec: 0027 products, 0029 step actions and 0025 alternatives and labels.
